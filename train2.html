The American Freedom Era

4449 was removed from display on December 14, 1974, and restored at Burlington Northern's Hoyt Street roundhouse in Portland and returned to operation April 21, 1975, wearing a special paint scheme of red, white, and blue. As part of the American Freedom Train, the engine pulled a display train around the most of the United States. Afterwards, 4449 pulled an Amtrak special, the Amtrak Transcontinental Steam Excursion. After nearly two years on the road, 4449 was returned to storage in Portland, this time under protective cover and not exposed to the elements.

The Golden Age Of 4449

  • Return to the Daylight Colors (1981): SP 4449 was repainted into its original "Daylight" color scheme for the inaugural Railfair at the California State Railroad Museum in Sacramento.
  • The Record-Breaking Excursion (1984): 4449 pulled an all-Daylight train from Portland to New Orleans and back to promote the World's Fair. Covering 7,477 miles, it became the longest steam train excursion in U.S. history.
  • Hollywood and Corporate Service (1986): It traveled to Hollywood to star in the movie Tough Guys and was used to pull official Southern Pacific business trains.
  • Historic 1989 Meet: SP 4449 made a famous side-by-side entrance into Los Angeles Union Passenger Terminal alongside Union Pacific 844 to celebrate the station's 50th anniversary.
  • Later Railfairs & Temporary Repaints (1991–2004): After returning to Sacramento's Railfair in 1991 and 1999, it was temporarily repainted black and silver in 2000 for a BNSF special, and then into its patriotic American Freedom Train colors in 2002 following 9/11.
  • Final Restoration Look (2004): In 2004, the locomotive was restored to its permanent Daylight paint scheme, specifically mimicking its "as delivered" 1941 appearance.

Famous SP 4449 Doubleheaders

Note: A doubleheader in railroading is a train pulled by two locomotives coupled together at the front

SP 4449 + SP 4447

On October 17, 1954 during the donation of Narrow-Gauge Steam Locomotive SP No. 18, No. 4449 and No. 4447 ran a historic doubleheader excursion train from Los Angeles to Owenyo, California, and back

During the 2005 NRHS convention, these two preserved Pacific Northwest steam locomotives ran together on the Western Star Excursion from Portland to Wishram and back.

SP 4449 + SP&S 700 (2005)

SP 4449 + Union Pacific 844 (2007)

One of the most famous modern 4449 doubleheader. The two streamlined 4-8-4 steam locomotives ran together during the Puget Sound Steam Special between Tacoma and Everett, Washington, on May 18–19, 2007. It was their first-ever doubleheading event.

Where is SP 4449 now?

Southern Pacific 4449 is curently housed at the Oregon Rail Heritage Center in Portland, Oregon for toursists and locals to see.
